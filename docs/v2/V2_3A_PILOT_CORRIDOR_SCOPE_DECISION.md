# GeoSlide-JK 2.0 — Checkpoint V2-3A Pilot Corridor Scope Decision

> **Document Version:** 2.3A.0  
> **Status:** Approved Scope Decision  
> **Target Branch:** `geoslide-jk-v2-product-redesign`

---

## 1. Evaluated Scopes

1. **Scope A: Jammu – Srinagar Complete NH-44 Corridor (~295–301 km)**
   - Covers the entire UT highway from Jammu plains through Chenani-Nashri, Ramban, Banihal tunnel, to Srinagar.
   - *Limitation:* Contains multiple plains sections in Samba, Jammu, and Pulwama with low landslide exposure where 500m slope screening adds low value.

2. **Scope B: Udhampur – Ramban – Banihal Mountain Pilot Corridor (74.88 km)**
   - Focuses strictly on the active mountain slope section crossing the Pir Panjal mountain range (Panthyal, Digdol, Battery Cheshma, Ramsu, Banihal).
   - *Advantage:* High landslide density, continuous slope exposure, 100% overlap with 100m COG susceptibility rasters, and maximum operational relevance for NHAI and Ramban DDMA.

---

## 2. Recommended & Adopted Scope

**Scope B: Udhampur – Ramban – Banihal NH-44 Mountain Pilot Corridor**
- **Verified Length:** **74.88 km** (74,875.83 m)
- **Verified 500m Segment Count:** **150 Segments** (`NH44-JK-0001` to `NH44-JK-0150`)
- **Origin:** Udhampur Pilot Sector (`75.5175°E, 33.5782°N`)
- **Destination:** Banihal / Anantnag Sector (`75.1744°E, 33.7171°N`)
- **Route Direction:** South to North
